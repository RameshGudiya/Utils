UK Skilled Worker Visa – Sponsorship Requirements

Source: UK Home Office – Register of Licensed Sponsors (Workers)

  • Employer Requirement: The employer must appear on the Register of Licensed Sponsors (Workers) maintained by the UK Home Office (last updated 27 March 2026).
  • Minimum Salary (2026): £41,700 per year or £17.13/hr – whichever is higher. Roles on the Immigration Salary List (ISL) may qualify at a lower threshold.
  • Skill Level: RQF Level 6 (degree-equivalent or above). Data Analyst / BI Developer / Data Visualisation Engineer all qualify under SOC codes 2135 & 3539.
  • SOC Codes Applicable:
    • SOC 2135 – IT Business Analysts, Architects and Systems Designers
    • SOC 2136 – Programmers and Software Development Professionals
    • SOC 3539 – Business Associate Professionals (Business Intelligence)
  • Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS): Employer assigns a CoS reference; candidate applies for Skilled Worker visa online.
  • English Language: From 8 January 2026 – Level B2 (CEFR) required for first-time applicants.
  • Visa Duration: Up to 5 years; renewable. Path to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) after 5 years.
  • Processing Time: 3–8 weeks (overseas: ~3–4 weeks; inside UK: ~8 weeks).
Important: Only employers appearing on the official Home Office Register may issue a Certificate of Sponsorship. Always verify the sponsor status at gov.uk/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers before applying.

  1. Verify Sponsor Status: Before applying, check the company at gov.uk/register-of-licensed-sponsors-workers. The CSV (10.4 MB, updated Mar 2026) lists all ~50,000 licensed sponsors by name and rating.
  2. Target Consulting Firms First: Accenture, Deloitte, PwC, KPMG and EY have the highest volume of sponsorship approvals and actively hire senior BI professionals with MicroStrategy + Tableau + Power BI stacks.
  3. Lead with MicroStrategy: MicroStrategy is a relatively rare and high-value skill – highlight it prominently. Around 970 MicroStrategy roles exist on LinkedIn UK alone.
  4. Salary Positioning: With 15 years of experience, target the £70,000–£90,000 band. This comfortably clears the £41,700 Skilled Worker visa threshold and keeps you in the senior/lead bracket.
  5. Use Dedicated Sponsorship Job Boards: Platforms that cross-reference the Home Office register – huntukvisasponsors.com, ukvisajobs.com, and sponsorcompanies.co.uk – filter only licensed sponsors.
  6. LinkedIn Filter: On LinkedIn UK search use filter "Visa sponsorship provided" under Job preferences. The 970 MicroStrategy results and 82 Visa-Sponsored Data Science London results are strong starting points.
  7. English Language: From 8 Jan 2026 the CEFR B2 requirement applies to first-time Skilled Worker applicants – ensure documentation is ready if applicable.
  8. Prioritise Permanent Roles: Most Skilled Worker visa sponsorships are for permanent, full-time positions (not day-rate contracts) under the current 2026 rules.
